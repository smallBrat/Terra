Scope

The categories that actually matter for individuals

Corporate carbon tracking tries to account for everything. Personal footprints come from a handful of categories — and those are where change happens.

Start your assessment
🚗

Transport & Commute

Your car, public transit, rideshares, and flights. Often the largest single category for urban residents.

High impact
🏠

Home Energy

Electricity, heating, cooling, and how your home is powered. Shaped by your grid, appliances, and daily habits.

Medium impact
🍽️

Food & Diet

Meat-heavy diets carry a real footprint. Shifting even two meals a week makes a measurable difference.

Medium impact
📦

Shopping & Deliveries

New goods, packaging, and delivery logistics. Batching and mindful buying reduces this more than most expect.

Lower impact
